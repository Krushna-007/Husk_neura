[ FAQS ]

ANSWERS TO YOUR COMMON QUESTIONS.

Got questions?
We've got answers.

What can I use HuskML for?

You can train image, text, audio, and video classification models for any project or research.

Is HuskML free to use?

Yes, HuskML offers a free plan with 5 models per month. Paid plans give you more models and data.

Do I need coding skills?

No coding required. Just upload your data, choose your model type, and let HuskML handle the training.

How accurate are the models?

Model accuracy depends on your data quality. Most users get good results with clean, labeled datasets.

What if I don’t like the result?

You can regenerate as many times as needed, or edit outputs directly to suit your needs.
